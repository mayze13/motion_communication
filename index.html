Before you book

What actually happens in the session?

You are guided through a specially configured architectural space at PEARL alongside other participants, while overhead tracking records how the group moves through it. A researcher briefs you fully first, and you are free to stop at any point.

I thought the study used EEG?

The study runs two kinds of session. EEG sessions are recruited separately, involve two visits on different days, and have their own screening. This page books crowd sessions only — no EEG, one visit.

Why the questionnaire?

Your answers are part of the science: they let us relate how people move through space to who they are. It takes about 15–20 minutes and must be completed before your place is confirmed. You can request your own results after the study.
